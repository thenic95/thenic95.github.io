### Rationale

I support setting the Net-Change-Limit (NCL) for 2025 at 350 000 000 000 000 lovelace (₳350 million), roughly reflecting the Treasury inflows recorded in 2024.

Over the longer term, the NCL should be implemented as a protocol parameter and defined as a percentage of the live Treasury balance rather than a fixed number. A percentage-based guardrail would scale automatically, enabling smoother withdrawals and preventing a scenario where a large, fixed cap, potentially hundreds of millions of ada, could be withdrawn in a single action once a Treasury withdrawal governance proposal is approved.
