### Rationale

I am voting NO on the governance action "2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 7.5M ₳ for community builders" (`gov_action1mmalzkcxpyn33t05hml2hxpwq0ffv6uu4mhe8sv7guz5nmm4afysqlmepzl`) due to a critical administrative flaw that makes it unworkable, as well as significant deficiencies in its overall structure.

The primary issue is that the budget's proposed administrative plan cannot be executed. The budget proposer has designated Intersect as the single administrator for all seven bundled proposals. However, one of the included proposers (Starstream) has explicitly and publicly stated, in their proposal submission on GovTools, that they do not consent to Intersect managing their funds.

This creates a dead end. Intersect's policy is to only administer funds for projects that provide explicit consent. Since a core component of this budget package withholds that consent, the entire administrative framework as proposed is invalid. A budget proposer cannot unilaterally assign an administrator to another party's project without their agreement.

Beyond this flaw, the budget itself is severely lacking in quality from a governance perspective:

1. No Administrative Guidelines: The proposal omits clear guidelines detailing how the 7.5M ₳ in treasury funds will be managed, disbursed, and overseen.

2. Lack of Accountability: There is no mention of independent audits, which are essential for ensuring transparency and the responsible use of community funds.

While some of the individual proposals within this bundle may be strong, they are contained within a budget that is procedurally flawed and administratively unworkable. Approving such a deficient proposal would be a disservice to the community and would set a poor precedent for treasury governance.
