### Rationale

As a DRep I vote **NO** on governance action `gov_action10k0unljvaej0kd89w7pn0zkgdx59c7xkl0x5q78dzvdtd73u0kmqq5xl5y5`, which seeks to set the Net Change Limit (NCL) for 2025 at **200 million ada**. While the Constitution permits adjusting the NCL at any time, doing so too frequently undermines confidence in Cardano’s fiscal policy.

A predictable NCL lets builders craft reliable budgets; without that stability many teams may hesitate to develop on Cardano.

The proposal also offers no rationale for why **200 million ada** would be superior to the current **350 million ada** limit.

For future NCL decisions, ideally starting with 2026, I recommend submitting several NCL info governance actions **simultaneously**, each covering the same period (1 January – 31 December) but with different withdrawal ceilings. Presenting, for example, five or six options that range from deficit spending to a potential surplus (relative to the previous 12 months of treasury inflows) would give DReps a more nuanced choice and help them record their preferences accurately.

If multiple NCL actions reach the constitutionally required >50 % active DRep stake at expiry, the action that secures the **highest number of YES votes** should be considered binding.
