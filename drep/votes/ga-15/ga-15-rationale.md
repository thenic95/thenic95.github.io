### Rationale

I am voting **NO** on the governance action "Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget" (gov_action1fpqwxp2kxvnntr8hpkh9q9djm78ccdww7qlhg5safugh4stmcwzqql5lauu) due to significant concerns regarding its financial scope, governance structure, and overall feasibility.

The request for 50 million ada, which represents over 14% of the current Net Change Limit, is exceptionally large. Such a substantial allocation of community funds is not justified by the proposal's contents, which lack clearly defined goals, key performance indicators (KPIs), and a tangible projection of the return on investment for the treasury. Committing this amount of ada without a clear measure of success or benefit presents an unacceptable level of risk.

Furthermore, the governance framework outlined is insufficient. The reliance on non-binding "info actions" for crucial decisions is concerning. It proposes placing a considerable amount of ada under the control of a committee without robust accountability mechanisms or the rigorous oversight for withdrawals mandated by the Cardano Constitution (Art. IV, Sec. 2). This structure fails to provide adequate safeguards for the responsible management of treasury funds.

The proposal also suffers from several practical and technical shortcomings. The justifications provided for the very large budget are not compelling. Additionally, there is a metadata hash mismatch, the proposed timeline is vague and lacks detail, and the reliance on a basic multi-sig wallet instead of exploring more secure and transparent smart contract-based solutions is a missed opportunity for enhanced security and accountability.

In its current form, the proposal has too many fundamental weaknesses in its financial planning, oversight structure, and technical implementation. It requires a comprehensive revision to address these critical issues before it could be considered a responsible use of treasury resources.
