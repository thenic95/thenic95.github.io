## Reports and Official Documents

- **Voltaire Report 2023**  
  - *Description*: An overview of Cardano's governance progress and future directions.  
  - *Link*: [Voltaire Report 2023](https://forum.cardano.org/t/voltaire-report-navigating-the-new-age-of-cardano-governance/120660)

- **CIP-1694 Initial Governance Settings Report**  
  - *Description*: Outlines the initial governance parameter settings for Cardano Mainnet, developed by the Governance Parameter Working Group.

- **Delegate-Endorsed Cardano Constitution Draft**  
  - *Description*: The draft constitution endorsed by delegates at the Constitutional Conventions in Buenos Aires and Nairobi.

- **Cardano Governance Parameter Overview Report**  
  - *Description*: A guide explaining the governance parameters introduced with CIP-1694 and their role in Cardano's on-chain governance.  
  - *Link*: [Cardano Governance Parameter Overview](https://github.com/thenic95/cardano-governance/blob/main/Reports/Cardano%20Governance%20Parameter/cardano-governance-parameter-overview.md)
