## Educational Content
- **Webinar: Fundamentals of Cardano Governance**  
  - *Description*: A webinar covering the basics of Cardano governance, including the governance roles (DReps, SPOs and CC), the seven governance action types, and the decision-making process in Cardano.  
  - *Link*: [Fundamentals of Cardano Governance](https://www.youtube.com/live/y2kTmw6rdTw?feature=shared)

- **Masterclass: Mastering Protocol Parameters**  
  - *Description*: Learn about the importance of protocol parameters, their categorization (updatable and non-updatable), and how they shape Cardano's decentralized governance model. Diving into the roles of DReps, SPOs, and the Constitutional Committee, while providing actionable insights, tools, and resources to empower your governance participation.  
  - *Link*: [Mastering Protocol Parameters](https://youtu.be/eY3G1_lCqio?feature=shared)
